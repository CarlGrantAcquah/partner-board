FAQ

Straight answers.

Fees
What does it cost to find an expert?+
A small matching fee, which we'll tell you before you commit. Then you pay the specialist's price for the job itself, agreed upfront.
Is the matching fee refundable?+
No. It's our thanks for the matching, and it secures your specialist's contact. Instead of a refund, every job through the board comes with an exclusive partner bonus: a gift card, a discount or a bundle.
What do partners pay?+
A 20% referral fee on jobs we send them, only when a job closes. There's no fee to be listed. Partners who refer work to other partners earn 20% the same way.
Payment
How does payment work?+
Once the job is done, you pay securely through Grant Studios. We then pay your specialist their share. You deal with one trusted point of payment, and your specialist knows they'll be paid.
When do I pay for the job itself?+
After the work is done. The only thing you pay at the start is the small matching fee.
Vetting
How are specialists vetted?+
Before anyone is listed, we review their past work in their niche and talk with them directly. They also agree to the board's terms, including the client bonus. After each job we collect a testimonial, so the board keeps learning who delivers.
Why only SEO right now?+
Because we'd rather be the best place to get vetted SEO experts than an average place for everything. Other niches will follow once SEO is working well.
I'm an SEO specialist. How do I join?+
Email acquahgrantcarl@gmail.com with your speciality, a few examples of your work, and the bonus you'd offer board clients. We're inviting founding partners now.
